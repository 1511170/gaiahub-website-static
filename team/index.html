Core team

Gaia Agency.

The global agency serving the people and projects of the Gaia Network. Distributed teams, organised by specialty, that carry out the ecosystem’s operation and management.

diversity_3

Among these teams are lawyers, administrators, architects, engineers, farmers, economists, educators, communicators, psychologists and health specialists, alongside other trades, knowledge, skills and talents useful to the projects of the network. The agencies act mainly at the national level, in coordination with the global, local and project levels.
